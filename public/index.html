    How to Play

    Six steps. Sixty seconds to learn. A whole night to argue about.

    Cookout Card

    Watch the loop: the Leader locks a pick in secret, the crew votes, the tally talks.

    1
    Get the Crew In
    One person starts a room. Everybody else joins with the 4-letter code. Any phone works. No downloads, no sign-ups.
    2
    The Leader Picks a Plate
    Each round has a Leader. A card drops with 4 choices. The Leader locks in one pick. In secret. Nobody sees it.
    3
    Everybody Votes
    The crew votes for the one they ride with. Are you voting your heart, or reading the Leader's mind? That's the game.
    4
    Coins Change Everything
    You hold 3 coins, once each, all game. 🗣️ FILIBUSTER: 60 seconds to talk the table around, then everybody re-votes. 🔀 SWITCH SIDES: move your vote before the reveal. 📞 PHONE A FRIEND: send a poll link to somebody outside the game and steal their answer.
    5
    Score Like You Mean It
    The reveal: if the crowd's top pick matches the Leader, the Leader banks 3. Match the crowd? +1. Match the Leader? +1. Match both? That's 2. Stranded alone on a pick? That's Man on an Island — switch before the reveal or own it.
    6
    First to 7 Eats First
    Hit the target and the game ends. Tied at the top? LIGHTNING ROUND. One card. Winner takes the plate.

    Arguing is not cheating. Arguing is the game.
